# Role Management

**Related Use Cases:** [Manage Admin Roles](../UseCases/Admin/01-admin-portal-access.md#uc-ad-002)

## Functional Requirements

### REQ-ROLE-001: Role Definition
- Define user roles (General User, Admin, Super Admin)
- Assign specific permissions to each role
- Role hierarchy and inheritance
- Custom role creation capabilities

### REQ-ROLE-002: Permission Management
- Granular permission controls
- Permission assignment and revocation
- Role-based access to features and data
- Permission inheritance and overrides

### REQ-ROLE-003: Role Administration
- Assign/remove roles from user accounts
- Role change notifications and confirmations
- Role audit trail and history
- Bulk role management operations

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Role changes take effect immediately
- Role management is intuitive and secure
- All role changes are logged
- Role system scales with user growth

## Acceptance Criteria
- Role system is flexible and secure
- Role management is efficient and reliable
- All role changes are auditable
- Role permissions are clearly defined and enforced
