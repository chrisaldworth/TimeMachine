# Content Curation

**Related Use Cases:** [Curate Featured Content](../UseCases/Admin/04-upload-operations.md#uc-ad-010)

## Functional Requirements

### REQ-CURATE-001: Featured Content Management
- Mark content as featured or highlighted
- Create curated collections and galleries
- Set content priority and visibility levels
- Manage content placement and organization

### REQ-CURATE-002: Collection Management
- Create themed collections of photos
- Organize content by topic, location, or time period
- Set collection visibility and access controls
- Collection sharing and promotion tools

### REQ-CURATE-003: Content Promotion
- Highlight important historical content
- Create educational and thematic displays
- Promote user-generated content
- Content recommendation and discovery

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Content curation tools are intuitive and efficient
- Featured content loads quickly and prominently
- Collections are well-organized and discoverable
- Curation doesn't impact system performance

## Acceptance Criteria
- Admins can effectively curate and promote content
- Featured content enhances user experience
- Collections are well-organized and accessible
- Content curation tools are powerful and flexible
