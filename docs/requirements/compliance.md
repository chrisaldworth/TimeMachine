# Compliance

**Related Use Cases:** [View Audit Logs](../UseCases/Admin/05-security-compliance.md#uc-ad-011), [Enforce Content Policies](../UseCases/Admin/05-security-compliance.md#uc-ad-012)

## Functional Requirements

### REQ-COMP-001: Data Privacy Compliance
- GDPR compliance for EU users
- Data export and deletion capabilities
- Privacy policy and terms of service
- Cookie consent and tracking controls

### REQ-COMP-002: Legal Compliance
- DMCA takedown process and procedures
- Copyright protection and attribution
- Content licensing and usage rights
- Age verification and COPPA compliance

### REQ-COMP-003: Audit and Reporting
- Comprehensive audit logs for all actions
- Compliance reporting and documentation
- Data retention and deletion policies
- Legal hold and evidence preservation

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Compliance measures are automated where possible
- Audit logs are tamper-proof and timestamped
- Compliance reporting is accurate and timely
- Legal requirements are met without impacting user experience

## Acceptance Criteria
- Platform meets all applicable legal requirements
- User privacy is protected according to regulations
- Compliance procedures are documented and followed
- Audit trails support legal and regulatory requirements
